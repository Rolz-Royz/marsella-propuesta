What you told us, and where it went

It should feel like a hall, not a warehouse.

That is the headline of the home page and the venue page.

A rustic style, unlike other Latino venues.

The whole design is warm and light: whitewashed wood, brick, hand-painted red and green.

Prices depend on the date, guests and add-ons.

No prices. The “Build your party” menu lets visitors tick food, DJ, bartender, waitstaff and extras, and it all lands in the quote.

We are a five-year team; half of us are family.

A team section on the home page (“Five years of parties, together”) and an About us page.

We want the English market, Tuesday to Thursday.

A dedicated weeknight page, English at the root and English copy written for that audience.

Muñequitos: just a mention and a click.

One line in every footer and a minimal page.

The idea: the party menu.

The site takes its cue from the hand-painted menu boards of Mexican fondas, refined for an elegant hall: a whitewashed-wood ground, lettering painted in red and green, and a board listing every service and extra.

Each visitor ticks what they want on the board (food, DJ, Maruchan bar, hora loca…). Their picks are saved as they browse and show up by themselves in the quote form. You get complete requests instead of just “how much?”.

What we still need

Everything the site marks [To confirm] comes from this list. We never invent prices, rules or reviews.

  • Team photo: and the names of those who want to appear.
  • Tour hours: days and times to see the hall.
  • WhatsApp: do you use it, and on which number?
  • Photo mirror: do you offer it yet?
  • Deposit: amount, timing and cancellation policy.
  • Outside vendors: cake, photographer, décor, food.
  • Closing time: and how long an event runs.
  • Kitchen: is there one for the banquet?
  • Floor plan: measurements and table layouts.
  • Parking: number of spaces.
  • Ceremonies: can a wedding ceremony happen in the hall?
  • Small parties: do you take under 100 guests?
  • Muñequitos: address, phone and capacity.
  • Logo: original or vector file.
  • Form: which email or number should receive quote requests.
  • Domain: is marsellaeventvenue.com yours?

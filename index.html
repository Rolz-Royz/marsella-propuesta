By day: brick, wood and greenery.

Marsella was born because families needed a hall for quinceañeras and weddings that truly feels like a reception hall, not a warehouse. We chose a rustic style, unlike any other Latino venue in the valley.

  • WallsBrick, with brick columns
  • CeilingHanging greenery and bistro lights
  • TablesWood farm tables, round and long, with cross-back chairs
  • LightFloor-to-ceiling windows
  • Capacity100 to 250 guests
  • OutsidePlenty of parking, no houses around us

And when night falls…

The lights come on, the LED floor glows and your name is on the screens. The same hall, a different party.

By night

  • LED dance floorA mirror floor in front of the stage, for the waltz and the first dance.
  • Your name on screenThe screens show names, photos or your company logo.
  • Lights and soundColor lighting and pro sound with the DJ.
  • Greenery and lightsThe hanging greenery and string lights glowing over the tables.

Rustic, warm and made for celebrating.

Marsella was born because families needed a hall made for quinceañeras and weddings that truly feels like a reception hall. We chose a rustic style, unlike any other Latino venue in the valley.

  • Brick, wood and greenery with string lights over the tables.
  • 100 to 250 guests at round and long farm tables.
  • Plenty of parking and no houses around us.
  • A stage, dance floor and screens for the first dance, the band or the toast.

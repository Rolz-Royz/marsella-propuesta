Our specialty

Her waltz, her throne, her night.

Marsella was built for quinceañeras. Her name on the screens, her waltz on the dance floor and her family in a hall that feels like a reception hall, not a warehouse.

  • The entrance: her name on the stage screens
  • The waltz: on the LED dance floor, in front of everyone
  • The throne: on the stage for the quinceañera
  • Hora loca: and a Maruchan bar or pozole to close the night

Two languages, two families

Elegant, rustic and bilingual.

Weddings with service in English and Spanish so both families feel at home. Brick, greenery and daylight for dinner; the LED floor and lights for the dance.

  • In two languages: a Spanish line and an English line
  • The sweetheart table: on the stage, among flowers
  • The first dance: with a DJ, sound and lighting
  • Bar and dinner: a bartender and waitstaff all night

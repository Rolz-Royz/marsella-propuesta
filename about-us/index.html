Our story

We have worked together for five years running event halls. We started with small ones, for up to 100 guests.

Marsella was born because families needed a hall made for quinceañeras and weddings, for 100 to 250 guests, that truly feels like a reception hall and not a warehouse. That is why we chose a rustic style, unlike any other Latino venue around.

Half of our team is family and the other half are friends and collaborators, not employees. You can tell in how we look after every party.

We serve you in English and Spanish.
